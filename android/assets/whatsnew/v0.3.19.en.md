A new mark and startup animation, a top bar folded down to a single button, and fixes for first install pulling in every module, the pinned-version picker, and weekday 7 in scheduled tasks.

## New mark and startup animation

- The mark is now three nested arcs with a dot in the middle — the c-c-c-o at the start of the name.
- On launch the arcs draw in from the outside in and the dot lights up last; while you wait, a glow sweeps outward. Once the page has loaded, the mark shrinks and fades away, with no blank flash in between.
- A normal launch shows just the mark and one line of progress. The full setup page only opens when something fails, needs downloading, or needs your choice.
- The welcome screen uses the same mark, and the three example prompts under the input box are gone — only Tips remains.

## Top bar

- The top right now shows a single ⋯. Tap it and Files, Terminal, Command palette and New session slide out, and ⋯ turns into ›; tap again to fold them away. The app remembers whether you left it open.
- The ❄ button is gone. If the page stops scrolling, **long-press the title in the top bar** (the session name); it dims, gives a short buzz, and the page is rebuilt.
- The elapsed time at the top of a reply no longer has a border — it's just a line of small text.

## Fixes

- First install could install all eight modules even when only two were ticked: the background update check kept running and pre-downloaded the full kit as an update. It now waits until first install has finished.
- Choosing "CLI: pinned version…" in Settings opened the version picker underneath the settings sheet, so it couldn't be used.
- Scheduled tasks mishandled 7 (Sunday) in the weekday field: `1-7` never ran and `0-7` meant Sunday only. (#16 — thanks for the report)
